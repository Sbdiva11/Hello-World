About SpongeBob

SpongeBob SquarePants is an endlessly optimistic sea sponge who resides in a pineapple under the sea at 124 Conch Street. He spends his days cooking Krabby Patties, practicing karate, and catching jellyfish in Jellyfish Fields.

Friends & Neighbors

Patrick Star

SpongeBob's best friend is a well-meaning pink starfish who lives under a rock and enjoys jellyfishing and sleeping.

Squidward Tentacles

SpongeBob's next-door neighbor and cashier coworker at the Krusty Krab, who plays the clarinet and longs for high-culture artistic fame.

The Krusty Krab

Owned by Eugene H. Krabs, the Krusty Krab is Bikini Bottom's premier eatery, famous for its legendary sandwich.
