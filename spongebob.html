About SpongeBob

SpongeBob SquarePants is an endlessly optimistic sea sponge who resides in a pineapple under the sea at 124 Conch Street. He spends his days cooking Krabby Patties at the Krusty Krab, practicing karate, and catching jellyfish in Jellyfish Fields.

The Krusty Krab

Owned by Eugene H. Krabs, the Krusty Krab is Bikini Bottom's premier eatery, famous for its legendary sandwich.

Krabby Patty Essentials

  1. Sesame seed buns
  2. Fresh sea lettuce and tomatoes
  3. Sea cheese and pickles
  4. Flame-broiled patty
  5. Secret Krabby Patty Sauce

Friends & Neighbors

Patrick Star

SpongeBob's best friend is a well-meaning pink starfish who lives under a rock and enjoys jellyfishing and sleeping.

Squidward Tentacles

SpongeBob's next-door neighbor and cashier coworker at the Krusty Krab, who plays the clarinet and longs for artistic fame.

Sandy Cheeks

A thrill-seeking, karate-chopping squirrel from Texas who lives in an airtight glass Treedome under the sea and works as a scientist.
